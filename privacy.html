Privacy Policy

Driver Ratings · Last updated 10 August 2026

Driver Ratings is a free mobile app that presents the FIA Driver Categorisation List in a searchable form. This policy explains what the app collects, what it does not, and what you can ask of us.

Driver Ratings is unofficial. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile. The FIA's own published list is the authority on any categorisation.

Who is responsible

Oliver Caldwell is responsible for this app and for the personal data described below. You can get in touch using the form at the bottom of this page, or the "Report a problem" screen inside the app.

The short version

  • There are no accounts and no logins.
  • We do not use analytics, and we do not track what you look at in the app.
  • We only receive personal information about you if you choose to send us a message.
  • The adverts in the app are supplied by Google, who do collect information. That is the main data collection involved.

Information you send us

If you use the report form, we receive the email address you enter, your subject and message, the date of the driver list you were viewing, and whether you were using an iPhone or an Android phone. The list date is included so a report about a wrong entry can be traced to the exact version you saw.

These messages are delivered through Formspree, a form-handling service, and then to our email. Your address is used only to reply to you. It is never sold, shared, or added to any mailing list.

Messages are kept for as long as they are useful for correcting the data, and deleted when they are not.

Advertising

Advertising is not yet active in the app. This section describes how it will work once it is introduced, and applies from that point onwards.

The app is free, and is supported by a small advertising banner. Adverts are supplied by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers, approximate location and information about how adverts are interacted with, in order to select and measure them. This collection is Google's, not ours, and is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

If you are in the UK or the European Economic Area, you will be asked for your choices about personalised advertising when you first open the app, and you can change them later. On iPhone, you will separately be asked whether to allow tracking, and you may decline.

The driver list

The app displays the FIA Driver Categorisation List, which contains the names, nationalities and categorisations of licensed racing drivers. That information is published by the FIA and is already public. This app reproduces it so that it can be read and searched on a phone.

The list is downloaded to your phone and stored there so the app works without a signal. It is not linked to you in any way, and we cannot tell who has looked at what.

If you are a driver on this list and believe your entry is wrong, or you object to it appearing here, please contact us using the form below. We will respond. Please note that a categorisation itself can only be changed by the FIA, and any correction to the underlying data has to come from them.

What we never collect

  • Your name, unless you put it in a message to us
  • Your location, beyond whatever Google uses to select adverts
  • Your contacts, photos, calendar, microphone or camera
  • Any record of which drivers you searched for or looked at

Your rights

If you are in the UK or the European Economic Area, you have the right to ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to how it is used. In practice, the only personal data we are likely to hold about you is a message you sent us.

To exercise any of these rights, use the form below. You also have the right to complain to your data protection regulator; in the UK this is the Information Commissioner's Office.

Children

This app is intended for people involved in or following motorsport professionally. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. Significant changes will be noted in the app.

Contact

Use this form for anything in this policy, including a request about your own data, or a correction to a driver entry.
